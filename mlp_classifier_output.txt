Explanation: Yes. The larger network fits the training data better while
keeping test accuracy at least as high, so it is the better model. Three
hidden neurons are a tight bottleneck for separating versicolor from
virginica, whose measurements overlap; more hidden units give the network
capacity to model that boundary. Gains are small because Iris is easy and
the tuned 1 x 3 network was already near the ceiling. More is not always
better: a deeper network can fail to train at all with the same plain-SGD
settings (see any row stuck at 33.33%, i.e. predicting a single class).
